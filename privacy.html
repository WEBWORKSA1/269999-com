Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (name, email, optional phone, numbers or dates you ask us to analyse, messages, contest entries).
  • Tool inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.
  • Usage data via cookies from analytics and advertising partners, if enabled.

How we use it

To answer requests, deliver reports, run contests, process donations and send newsletters you opted into. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form relay (FormSubmit). Please don't submit sensitive information such as payment card numbers or government IDs.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

YouTube embeds

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load after you click play.

Local storage

We store small preferences (theme, referral code) in your browser. You can clear them anytime.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, PIPEDA, Québec Law 25, CCPA), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data via our contact page (topic: Privacy request).

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are for adults only.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
