The number behind the name

Why 269999?

Read in Cantonese, 2-6 sounds like 易祿 (yi luk) — "easy fortune". 9999 is 久久久久 — "forever and ever", the same wish behind 999 roses and ¥9,999 wedding gifts. Together: easy fortune, lasting forever.

We're honest about readings too: in Mandarin, 二六 can echo 二流 ("second-rate"). Every tool here shows both sides — that's what makes it trustworthy.

Frequently asked questions

What are the luckiest numbers in Chinese culture?

8 (八, sounds like 发 "prosper"), 6 (六, "smooth"), and 9 (九, sounds like 久 "long-lasting") are the classic trio. 2 is positive because good things come in pairs.

Why is 4 considered unlucky?

四 (sì) sounds almost identical to 死 (sǐ, "death"). Many buildings in China skip the 4th floor, and numbers containing 4 — especially 14 ("want to die") — are avoided.

How does the analyzer calculate a score?

It weighs each digit's traditional meaning, detects famous combinations (168, 520, 1314, 8888…), applies the classic 81-number method to the last four digits, and checks the Five Elements balance. See our methodology.

Is this fortune-telling?

No. It's a cultural and linguistic guide for entertainment and education. Numbers don't guarantee outcomes — but understanding them helps you communicate, price and brand for Chinese audiences.
