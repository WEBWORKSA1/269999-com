Lucky Number Analyzer

Pick what you're checking. We read every digit in Mandarin & Cantonese, spot lucky and unlucky combinations, and apply the traditional 81-number method.

How the analyzer works

  1. Digit meanings. Each digit carries a sound-alike meaning: 8 ≈ 发 (prosper), 6 ≈ 顺/禄 (smooth/fortune), 9 ≈ 久 (lasting), 4 ≈ 死 (death).
  2. Famous combinations. Patterns like 168 (一路发), 518 (我要发), 520 (我爱你), 1314 (一生一世) and warnings like 14 (要死) and 748 (去死吧).
  3. 81-number method (八十一数理). Divide the last four digits by 80, keep the decimal, multiply by 80 — a popular traditional check for phone numbers.
  4. Five Elements. He Tu mapping reveals which element dominates your number.

Tips by use-case

Phone numbers

Endings matter most. A number ending in 8, 88, 168 or 9999 is easy to remember and signals success to clients.

License plates

Short plates with repeated 8s or 9s command auction premiums in Hong Kong, Singapore and mainland China.

Addresses

Avoid 4, 14, 24 where Chinese buyers are a big share of the market — it can affect resale.

Prices

Charm prices ending in 8 or 88 perform well with Chinese shoppers; avoid prices with 4.
