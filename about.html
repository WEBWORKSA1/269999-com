About 269999.com

We make Chinese number culture easy to understand — and useful — for everyone: couples picking a wedding date, founders naming a brand, families buying a home, and anyone curious why 8 is everywhere.

Why this name?

In Cantonese, 26 sounds like 易祿 ("easy fortune") and 9999 is 久久久久 ("forever"). Our wish for every visitor: easy fortune that lasts. We use the number purely as our web address and a descriptive theme.

Methodology

  1. Digit symbolism — each digit's Mandarin and Cantonese homophones (e.g. 8 ≈ 发, 4 ≈ 死), weighted and normalised for length.
  2. Combination library — 35+ well-documented patterns (168, 518, 520, 1314, 5201314, 14, 748, 250…), with longer patterns taking priority.
  3. 81-number method (八十一数理) — last four digits ÷ 80, remainder × 80, classified auspicious / mixed / challenging.
  4. Five Elements — He Tu mapping (1·6 Water, 2·7 Fire, 3·8 Wood, 4·9 Metal, 5·0 Earth).
  5. Almanac — day stem-branch from the Julian Day Number, simplified Twelve Day Officers relative to the solar month, and zodiac clash.

Scores run 3–99 and are for cultural, educational and entertainment purposes. Traditions vary by region and school; we show the caveats (like 26 ≈ 二流 in Mandarin) rather than hide them.
