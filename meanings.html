Mandarin vs Cantonese: why readings differ

Most homophones come from Mandarin, but Cantonese — spoken in Hong Kong, Guangdong and much of the diaspora — creates its own. 2 sounds like 易 (easy), so 28 is "easy prosperity" and 26 is "easy fortune" (易祿). In Mandarin, 26 can instead echo 二流 ("second-rate"). 9 is lucky in both, but also carries a vulgar Cantonese slang meaning in some contexts. Good analysis always considers the audience.

Cultural etiquette

  • Give red envelopes in even amounts with 8 or 6 (¥88, ¥168, ¥888). Avoid amounts with 4.
  • Wedding gifts often use 9 or 520/1314 for lasting love.
  • Don't give clocks (送钟 sounds like attending a funeral) — a number-adjacent faux pas!
  • Number 7: the 7th lunar month is the Ghost Month; many avoid weddings then.
